Featured Trail

This week Deepak recommends the Don Valley trail from Pottery Road to Crothers Woods. It is an easy loop with river views and a long boardwalk section. Plan about two hours to walk the full route.

Trail Conditions

The trail is mostly dry, with a few muddy spots after the Bayview underpass. Wear good shoes. The north entrance stairs are open again.

Deepak's Gear Tips

Bring a water bottle, a light jacket, and a small first aid kit. Check the weather before you leave and tell someone your route.
